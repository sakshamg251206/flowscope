OFI follows Cont, Kukanov & Stoikov (2014): each change in the best bid or ask adds the size that arrived and subtracts the size that left, depending on whether the price moved up, down, or stayed. Price changes are in ticks. A same-bucket fit explains price moves; it is not a trading signal, which is what the next-bucket R² shows.
